## Supplied brief

The three-page 7H brief supplies the working positioning (`Story-Driven Afro-Pop & Afro-Fusion`), the `Creator + Lover` archetype, earth-tone/linen styling with a beaded wristpiece, an ages 18–32 Kampala / East African diaspora audience, four objectives, a 12-week plan, outreach names and a `$1,500` budget scenario.

The four objectives I’ll work toward are **150,000+ new campaign-attributed streams in the 12-week window**, **2,500+ Inner Circle members**, **30+ East African DJs plus weekly plays on four major stations**, and **150 capsule merch units / `$2,250` gross plus one ticketed showcase**. `150,000+` means new campaign-period streams because `Badman` already had `311,906` all-time Spotify plays in the snapshot.

The budget scenario is `$1,500` allocated (`$600` content/video, `$400` radio/DJ/PR, `$250` TikTok/IG ads, `$250` merch), `$3,850` projected gross (`$2,250` merch, `$1,000` showcase, `$600` DSP royalties) and `+$2,350` projected net margin. I’ll confirm currency before spend, and I’ll treat these as forecast lines, not earnings.

Outreach lists — NRG Radio Uganda, 91.3 Capital FM, KIIS FM 100.9, NBS After 5, XFM and NTV Beat — are working contact leads. XFM is flagged as stopped in a directory and NTV Beat is a legacy show name; I’ll set current contacts and availability with the team before launch. No station, TV, playlist, DJ or venue booking is treated as secured.

## Before launch

I’ll set the exact 12-week dates, launch owner, budget currency, audio clearances, venue/ticketing terms and Week 0 private DSP baselines with the 7H team before launch. “2027” is the campaign heading; the review is due 25 Nov 2026.

I’ll treat wins, interviews, placements, bookings and revenue as future outcomes. I’ll leave unconfirmed handles and independent demographic research out of the plan. The SALO and `Do you?` cover cards are generated typographic placeholders.
